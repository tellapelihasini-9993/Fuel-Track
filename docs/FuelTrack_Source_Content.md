## 📑 Slide 3: Problem Analysis & Industry Inefficiencies
- **Header**: Critical Pain Points in Traditional Fuel Retail
- **Core Challenges**:
  1. **Urban Traffic Congestion & Lost Productive Hours**:
     - Urban petrol stations create severe bottleneck queues during peak traffic hours.
     - Commercial transport fleets and delivery vans lose an average of **45 minutes per trip** merely waiting to refuel.
  2. **Predatory Surge Pricing & Lack of Transparency**:
     - Existing third-party roadside breakdown assistance services charge predatory convenience markups (+30% to +50% above market price) during emergencies.
  3. **Severe Backup Generator Safety Hazard**:
     - Hospitals, telecom cell towers, data centers, and construction equipment use diesel generators that cannot drive to a pump.
     - Transporting fuel in unapproved plastic jerrycans in passenger vehicles violates PESO regulations and creates extreme fire and combustion hazards.
- **Speaker Notes**:
  > "Traditional fuel supply has three major issues: wasted time in long queues, predatory surge pricing during breakdowns, and dangerous handling of loose fuel canisters for standby generators."

## 📑 Slide 5: The Zero-Markup Pricing Model
- **Header**: Mathematical Price Formula & Comparative Breakdown
- **Core Formula**:
  $$\mathbf{Total\ (₹)} = (\mathbf{Litres} \times \mathbf{Official\ Daily\ Rate}) + \mathbf{Flat\ ₹50\ Delivery\ Fee} + \mathbf{₹0.00\ Extra}$$
- **Sample Bill: 35 Litres Petrol (Mumbai Region)**:
  - Official Petrol Unit Rate: **₹104.21 / Litre**
  - Fuel Subtotal ($35 \times 104.21$): **₹3,647.35**
  - PESO Safety Handling & Delivery Fee: **₹50.00**
  - Platform Surcharge / Surge Markup: **₹0.00 (Zero Markup)**
  - **Exact Payable Total**: **₹3,697.35**
- **Competitor Comparison**:
  - *FuelTrack*: Market Rate + Flat ₹50 + IoT calibrated flow ($\pm0.01\%$) + PESO density certificate.
  - *Third-Party Providers*: +20% to +40% surge price + ₹250–₹500 delivery fee + uncalibrated manual canisters.
- **Speaker Notes**:
  > "Our transparent formula guarantees customer trust: pay strictly for the fuel at official government rates plus a flat ₹50 safety delivery fee with zero surge charges."
