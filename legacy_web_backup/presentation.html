Problem Analysis

Critical Inefficiencies in Traditional Fuel Retail

Why current petrol pump infrastructure fails consumers, commercial fleets, and standby power

1. Severe Congestion & Idle Loss

Urban petrol stations suffer from severe bottleneck queues during peak morning and evening hours. Commercial fleets lose thousands of productive hours and burn excess fuel merely queueing up.

Impact: ~45 minutes wasted per vehicle trip, compounding city traffic gridlock.

2. Predatory Surge Pricing

Existing breakdown assistance and roadside refuelling services exploit emergencies by charging 30% to 50% above market price plus steep convenience charges and hidden commissions.

Impact: Consumers lack trust in doorstep fuel services due to unpredictable pricing.

3. Severe Generator Safety Hazard

Hospitals, data centers, residential complexes, and construction equipment use diesel generators that cannot be driven to pumps. Transporting fuel in unapproved plastic cans creates high fire hazards and violates PESO norms.

Impact: Direct PESO compliance violations, fuel spillage, and high combustion risks.
🎙️ Speaker Talking Points: The current model is outdated: people lose hours in pump traffic, roadside providers overcharge during emergencies, and handling fuel in loose jerrycans for backup generators creates serious safety hazards.
